Privacy Policy for Akuru (Sinhala Word Game)

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Akuru ("the app") is a local multiplayer word game published by APEXCORE ONE ("we", "us").
Contact: apexcoreone@gmail.com

Summary

Akuru does not have user accounts, servers, ads or analytics. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

What the app does with information

Player name. You type a name when you start or join a game. It is sent over your local network to the other players in the same game so they can see who you are. It is not sent to us or any server, and it is not stored after the app is closed.

Game answers, votes and chat messages. These are sent over your local Wi-Fi (or hotspot) to the players in the same game. They exist only for the duration of the game session and are not stored by us.

Network information. To create a Room Code, the app reads your device's local Wi-Fi IP address. The address is used only on your local network and is not transmitted to us.

Vibration. The app uses the vibration motor for feedback. No data is collected from it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not share data with third parties, because we do not receive any.

Chat and other players

Games are played with people you choose to play with. Chat messages are visible to every player in the game. You can block a player (hides their messages on your device) or report them (tells the host, who can remove them from the game) using the "Block / Report" link next to their messages. Please do not share personal information in chat.

Children

The app is a general-audience word game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Chat is between players in the same room; parents should supervise who their children play with.

Security

All game traffic stays on your local network between the devices in the game. It is not encrypted, so only play on networks you trust.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
