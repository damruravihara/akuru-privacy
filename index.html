Privacy Policy for Akuru (Sinhala Word Game)

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Akuru ("the app") is a multiplayer word game published by APEXCORE ONE ("we", "us").
Contact: apexcoreone@gmail.com

Summary

Akuru has no user accounts, ads or analytics. Wi-Fi games stay on your local network. If you choose to play online, your game connects to a server run by us, described below. We do not sell your data.

What the app does with information

Player name. You type a name when you start or join a game. In a Wi-Fi game it is sent over your local network to the other players in the same game so they can see who you are, and it is not sent to us. In an online game it is sent to our game server and the other players in your room (see "Online games"). The app remembers your last name on your device (see "Saved on your device" below).

Game answers, votes and chat messages. In Wi-Fi games these are sent over your local Wi-Fi (or hotspot) to the players in the same game. They exist only for the duration of the game session and are not stored by us. Chat is available in Wi-Fi games only (see "Chat and other players").

Network information. To create a Room Code for a Wi-Fi game, the app reads your device's local Wi-Fi IP address. The address is used only on your local network. For online games the server also sees your IP address, as described under "Online games".

Vibration. The app uses the vibration motor for feedback. No data is collected from it.

Saved on your device. To save you typing, the app stores your last player name, your game settings (rounds, timer, letter and voting modes), the last Room Code you joined, whether you have seen the How to Play screen, and, for a short time, the details needed to rejoin an online game you left by accident (the room code and a random rejoin key). This stays on your device only, is never sent to anyone except the game you are rejoining, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Notification while hosting. When you host a Wi-Fi game, Android shows an ongoing notification ("Akuru game in progress") so the game keeps running if you switch to another app. It contains no personal information and disappears when the game ends.

Online games

When you choose "Online", the app connects over the internet to our game server (hosted by APEXCORE ONE). To run the game the server receives your player name, the room code you create or join, your answers, votes and flags, and your IP address (which every internet connection reveals). This information is kept in the server's memory only while your game is running and is discarded when the game ends or the room closes, at the latest after 15 minutes without activity. It is shared only with the other players in your room (your name, answers and votes). We do not store it in a database, build profiles, or use it for advertising or analytics.

Server logs. Our web server keeps standard access logs that include IP addresses, and our game server records IP addresses when it refuses abusive traffic. We use these only to keep the service secure and working, and delete them after 14 days.

Player names and reports. Player names are the only text other players see in an online game. The server refuses names that contain blocked words, links, email addresses or phone numbers. You can report another player's name from the lobby; the report is sent to the host of the game, who can remove that player. Reports are not stored.

No accounts. There is no sign-in. The server gives your device a random, temporary id and rejoin key for the length of the game.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, other than the other players in your game.

Chat and other players

Games are played with people you choose to play with. In Wi-Fi games, chat messages are visible to every player in the game. You can block a player (hides their messages on your device) or report them (tells the host, who can remove them from the game) using the "Block / Report" link next to their messages. Please do not share personal information in chat. Chat is not available in online games in this version.

Children

The app is a general-audience word game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Online games have no chat and no accounts. Wi-Fi chat is between players in the same room; parents should supervise who their children play with.

Security

Online traffic is encrypted. Wi-Fi game traffic stays on your local network between the devices in the game and is not encrypted, so only play Wi-Fi games on networks you trust.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
